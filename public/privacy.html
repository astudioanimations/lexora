Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 July 2026

Lexora is a word puzzle game. This policy explains what we collect and why. We keep it minimal on purpose.

What we collect

  • Account info — if you sign in, we store your email (and, for Google, your name and profile image) to create your account.
  • Game progress — your score, current level, and bonus words, so progress syncs across your devices.

If you play without signing in, your progress stays only on your device and is never sent to us.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data.
  • We don't show third-party ads in this version.
  • We don't track you across other websites.

Service providers

  • Cloudflare — hosting and database storage.
  • Google — optional sign-in (OAuth).
  • Resend — sending your sign-in emails.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account and all cloud-saved progress at any time from the in-game account menu → Delete my data.
