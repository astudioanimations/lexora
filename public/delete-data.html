Delete Your Account and Data

Lexora · AStudio Animations · Last updated: 24 July 2026

You can delete your Lexora account and all associated data at any time. There are two ways to do it.

Option 1 — Delete in the app (instant)

  1. Open Lexora.
  2. Tap the account button in the top-right corner.
  3. In the menu, tap "Delete my data."
  4. Confirm. Your account and all saved progress are permanently removed.

Deletion is immediate for the in-app option and permanent — it cannot be undone. We do not retain backups of deleted account data beyond routine, short-term system backups that are overwritten in the normal course of operation.

Playing without an account

If you play Lexora without signing in, your progress is stored only on your device and is never sent to us. You can remove it by clearing the app's data in your device settings, or by uninstalling the app.
